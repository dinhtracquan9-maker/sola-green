# Market editorial rollout

Status: research plan, not a claim of supply eligibility or keyword demand.

| Markets | Initial language | Buyer decision to research | Required evidence before publication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not clone a generic article and substitute country names. Each market brief needs a different buyer question, applicable primary sources, a real product set and confirmed operational information. Add hreflang only when equivalent translated pages exist. No invented delivery times, approvals, prices or distributor claims.

Measure qualified quote requests by country and product alongside Search Console impressions, clicks and indexing. Use confirmed fulfilment capability and qualified-lead results to set priorities; this table is not a market-size ranking.

## Legacy article review

Prioritize articles with generic procurement sections, unsupported composition/efficacy claims, references unrelated to the named product and overlapping search intent. Preserve URLs when rewriting. Consolidate only after comparing content and search performance. Do not mass-delete or mass-noindex without URL-level evidence.

SOLA comparison requires matching article URLs and dated evidence; similar homepage wording alone cannot establish copying direction.
