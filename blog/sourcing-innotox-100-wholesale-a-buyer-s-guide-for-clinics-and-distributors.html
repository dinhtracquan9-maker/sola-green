Understanding Botulinum Toxin Products

Botulinum toxin products, including Innotox 100, are used primarily for cosmetic purposes. They work by temporarily paralyzing the muscles that cause wrinkles, providing a smoother appearance. Understanding the mechanism of action and the various formulations available is essential for clinics and distributors to make informed choices.

Different botulinum toxin products may have varying potencies, dilutions, and recommended dosages. Familiarizing yourself with these differences will help you cater to the specific needs of your clientele, ensuring customer satisfaction and safety.

Regulatory Compliance and Documentation
