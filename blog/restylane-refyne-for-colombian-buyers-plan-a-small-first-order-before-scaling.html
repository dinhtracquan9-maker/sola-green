Defining Your First Order Acceptance Plan

Before placing a larger order for Restylane Refyne, it’s crucial to define a first-order acceptance plan. This plan should include specific criteria that a supplier must meet to ensure that your investment is sound. Here are the key components to consider:

  • Product Quality: Ensure the product meets your clinic’s standards. Request samples if possible.
  • Supplier Reliability: Assess the supplier's track record regarding delivery times and product availability.
  • Pricing Structure: Understand the pricing tiers and any discounts available for bulk orders.
  • Customer Support: Evaluate the level of support provided by the supplier, including training for your staff.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Confirm that the supplier adheres to local regulations for dermal fillers.

Checklist for Supplier Engagement

When engaging with suppliers for your first order of Restylane Refyne, consider the following checklist to ensure all bases are covered:

  • Have I requested and reviewed product samples?
  • What are the supplier's delivery timelines?
  • Is there a clear pricing structure with potential discounts?
  • What kind of training or support does the supplier provide?
  • Have I verified the supplier’s compliance with local regulations?

Key Questions for Your Supplier

To ensure a thorough understanding of your potential supplier, consider sending them the following questions:

  • Can you provide references from other Colombian resellers?

  • How often do you restock your inventory for Restylane Refyne?
