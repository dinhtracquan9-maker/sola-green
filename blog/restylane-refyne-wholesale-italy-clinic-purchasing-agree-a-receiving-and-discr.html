Understanding the Order-to-Invoice Matching Process

Order-to-invoice matching is a critical step in the procurement process. It ensures that what was ordered matches what is billed, preventing financial discrepancies and ensuring accountability. For Restylane Refyne, it is essential to confirm that the quantities, product codes, and any other specifications align with the initial purchase order.

Key Steps in Order-to-Invoice Matching

  • Verify the purchase order against the delivery receipt.
  • Check the invoice details against both the purchase order and the delivery receipt.
  • Ensure that any agreed-upon discounts or terms are accurately reflected in the invoice.

Managing Discrepancies Effectively

Discrepancies can arise during the receiving process, whether due to incorrect quantities, damaged goods, or missing items. Establishing a clear procedure for addressing these issues is vital for maintaining good supplier relationships and ensuring clinic operations run smoothly.

Steps to Address Discrepancies

  • Document the discrepancy immediately using a discrepancy notification form.
  • Contact the supplier promptly to report the issue, providing all necessary documentation.
  • Follow up to ensure that the issue is resolved in a timely manner.

Sample Discrepancy Notification

When notifying a supplier about a discrepancy, clarity is key. Below is a template you can use:

  • Date: ____________________
  • Supplier Name: ____________________
  • Order Number: ____________________
  • Description of Discrepancy: ____________________
  • Action Requested: ____________________
  • Contact Information: ____________________

Hypothetical Example of Discrepancy Management

Let’s consider a hypothetical example to illustrate the process. Imagine your clinic ordered 100 units of Restylane Refyne. Upon delivery, you receive only 90 units, and one of the boxes is damaged. Here's how you would handle this situation:

  • Upon delivery, you check the receiving checklist and note that you ordered 100 units but received only 90.
  • You also observe that one of the boxes has visible damage.
  • Immediately, you fill out the discrepancy notification form, documenting the missing units and the damaged box.
  • You contact the supplier, providing them with the order number, details of the discrepancy, and a request for a replacement for the damaged box and the missing 10 units.
  • After following up, the supplier acknowledges the issue and agrees to send the remaining units and a replacement box within the next week.

Supplier Escalation Before Payment

In the event of unresolved discrepancies, it is important to have an escalation process in place. Before making any payments, ensure that all issues have been addressed satisfactorily. This protects your clinic from potential financial losses and maintains the integrity of your procurement process.

Questions to Send to Your Supplier

As a purchasing coordinator, consider these questions to clarify any uncertainties with your supplier:

  • Can you provide confirmation of the order details as they were processed?
  • What is your standard procedure for addressing discrepancies?
  • How do you handle returns for damaged or incorrect products?
  • What is the expected timeframe for resolution of discrepancies?
