How to build a more efficient wholesale product request

A precise request reduces back-and-forth and helps your supplier return a useful quotation faster.

HANSEONG BEAUTY GLOBAL Editorial Team • Updated June 2026

“Please send your price list” sounds simple, but it often produces a less useful answer than a structured request. Wholesale pricing and shipping may depend on product mix, quantity and destination.

Use exact product names

Brands often have multiple variants, strengths or pack configurations. Copy the exact catalogue name and include the variant wherever possible.

Add a quantity to every line

Even an approximate quantity helps. If you are comparing volume options, state that clearly — for example, “quote 10 and 25 units.”

A strong request includes
  • Your name and business type
  • Destination country and city
  • Exact product and variant
  • Quantity per product
  • Target order date
  • Questions about availability or shipping

Separate priorities from alternatives

Mark essential items, optional items and acceptable alternatives. This allows the supplier to respond constructively if one requested product is unavailable.

Ask for a complete commercial picture

Clarify what the quotation includes and how long it remains valid. Product availability, carrier charges and exchange conditions can change, so avoid treating an old quote as a permanent price list.

Keep one written conversation

Where possible, keep product changes and confirmations in one message thread. This creates a clear record for both sides and reduces avoidable misunderstandings.

Use a line-item RFQ that can be reconciled

Give every requested item a line number. Record the catalogue name, requested presentation, quantity, whether substitution is permitted and any unresolved question. Ask the supplier to reply against those same line numbers. A quoted quantity should refer to a clearly defined unit: a box, vial or syringe must not be treated as interchangeable.

  • Line 01: exact item and presentation; requested quantity; unit definition to confirm.
  • Substitution: not permitted unless separately approved in writing.
  • Availability: confirmed quantity, expected confirmation date or unavailable.
  • Commercial details: currency, quotation validity and charges included or excluded.

Example: compare quantity options without changing the product

In a hypothetical request, a clinic asks for 10 and 25 boxes of the same item. The buyer should ask for two separately labelled options with the same presentation and destination. If one option uses a different pack configuration, the unit prices are not directly comparable. Record the number of saleable units only after the pack contents are confirmed; leave freight, tax and other unknown charges uncalculated until quoted.

Control changes before confirming the order

Number each revision of the request. If quantities change, ask for a replacement summary rather than relying on messages scattered through a conversation. Before payment, reconcile the final product list with the quotation, identify unresolved items and confirm who approves substitutions. Keep the accepted version for receiving checks.
