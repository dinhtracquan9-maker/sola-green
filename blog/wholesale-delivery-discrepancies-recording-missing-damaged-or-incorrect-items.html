Wholesale Delivery Discrepancies: Recording Missing, Damaged or Incorrect Items

Learn how to document wholesale delivery discrepancies effectively with our detailed guide, including checklists and hypothetical scenarios.

HANSEONG BEAUTY GLOBAL Editorial Team · November 06, 2026

This article provides a comprehensive approach to documenting wholesale delivery discrepancies, complete with a checklist and a hypothetical example for resellers.

Understanding Wholesale Delivery Discrepancies

In the world of procurement, particularly for resellers, wholesale delivery discrepancies can lead to significant operational challenges. These discrepancies may manifest as missing, damaged, or incorrect items delivered to your business. It's crucial to document these issues accurately to maintain effective communication with suppliers and ensure a smooth resolution process.

Importance of Documenting Discrepancies

Documenting an order discrepancy serves multiple purposes. It not only helps in tracking issues for future reference but also assists in maintaining a professional relationship with your suppliers. By providing clear and concise reports, you can facilitate discussions regarding potential resolutions or adjustments in future orders. This diligence can also impact your inventory management and customer satisfaction, as unresolved discrepancies can lead to stockouts or delays in fulfilling customer orders.

Creating a Discrepancy Report

When faced with a delivery discrepancy, the first step is to create a detailed discrepancy report. This report should include the following essential elements:

  • Order Number: Reference the specific order to which the discrepancy pertains.
  • Date of Delivery: Note the delivery date for context.
  • Item Details: List the items that are missing, damaged, or incorrect.
  • Condition of Items: Describe the condition of the items received.
  • Photographic Evidence: Attach photos of the items to support your claims.
  • Contact Information: Provide your contact details for follow-up.

By including these elements, your report will be clear and informative, making it easier for suppliers to understand the issue. Ensure that your report is factual and devoid of emotional language to maintain professionalism.

Hypothetical Example of a Discrepancy Report

Hypothetical Scenario: Imagine you are a reseller who ordered 100 units of a popular skincare product. Upon receiving the shipment, you find that 10 units are damaged and 5 units are the incorrect variant.

Your discrepancy report might look like this:

  • Order Number: 123456
  • Date of Delivery: October 15, 2023
  • Missing Items: None
  • Damaged Items: 10 units of Product A (photos attached)
  • Incorrect Items: 5 units of Product B (ordered Product C)
  • Condition of Items: Damaged items have visible dents and broken seals.
  • Contact Information: John Doe, Reseller Operations Manager, johndoe@example.com

This report provides a clear and concise overview of the issues encountered, making it easier for the supplier to address the discrepancies.

Checklist for Receiving Deliveries

To prevent discrepancies from occurring in the first place, having a receiving checklist can be invaluable. Here’s a basic checklist you can use upon receiving deliveries:

  • Verify Order Against Invoice: Check that the items match the invoice provided.
  • Inspect Packaging: Look for any visible damage to the packaging.
  • Count Items: Ensure the number of items matches the order.
  • Check Expiration Dates: For beauty products, ensure items are within their shelf life.
  • Document Any Issues: Take photos and notes of any discrepancies immediately.

Utilizing this checklist can help you catch discrepancies before they escalate, ensuring that your inventory remains accurate and your operations run smoothly.

Questions to Send to Your Supplier

If you encounter a discrepancy, here are some questions you might consider sending to your supplier:

  • Can you confirm the order details for shipment number [insert shipment number]?
  • What is your process for handling discrepancies like missing or damaged items?
  • How will you ensure that this issue does not occur in future orders?
  • What documentation do you require from our end to process this discrepancy?
  • How long will it take to resolve this issue, and what steps will you take?

By asking these questions, you can clarify the next steps and ensure a more efficient resolution process. This proactive communication can also help build a stronger partnership with your supplier.

Conclusion

Documenting wholesale delivery discrepancies is a vital task for resellers and procurement professionals. By creating a thorough discrepancy report, utilizing a receiving checklist, and communicating effectively with suppliers, you can navigate these challenges more efficiently.
