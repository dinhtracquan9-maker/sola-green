Supplier Evaluation Criteria

When evaluating suppliers for Restylane Refyne, US professional buyers should consider several factors:

  • Supply Chain Transparency: Assess the supplier's ability to provide detailed information about their sourcing and distribution processes.
  • Documentation and Certifications: Request documentation that proves the supplier's eligibility to distribute Restylane Refyne.
  • Reputation and References: Look for testimonials or case studies that highlight the supplier's track record in the industry.
